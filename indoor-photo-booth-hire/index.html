In the digital age, everyone loves taking photos and selfies with friends and family. It brings people together and puts a smile on everyone’s face. Bring the fun of a photo booth to your next big event or special occasion and make it a hit with your guests.

Say cheese with an Indoor Photo Booth

Whether you are planning a wedding reception or a corporate event, an Indoor Photo Booth brings entertainment and laughter to any venue. Guests gather with their friends, take one, double or triple prints and choose the photos they want to keep. Picture layouts and colour or black-and-white options let you tailor the keepsakes to your occasion.

Our team provides a box of colourful and hilarious props, including hats, glasses and masks. The booth is easy to use: press a button, strike a pose and make some memories. Guests can make unlimited visits and print as many photos as they want. After the event, you can enjoy a private web gallery with all the high-resolution photos and download the images you want to keep.

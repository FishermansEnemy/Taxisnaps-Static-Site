FAQ’s

How does the booth get to our event?

We bring the booth to your venue, set it up and dismantle it after the event. An operator looks after the booth and your guests during the hire.

How does the booth work?

Guests use the touchscreen to start a photo session. The camera takes a series of pictures, which appear on the viewing screen before the prints are produced.

How much space does the booth need?

The traditional booth needs approximately 2m × 1m alongside a wall. The Taxi Photo Booth is approximately 5m × 2m. Please confirm venue access and available space with us when you enquire.

How long can I hire the booth for?

Our packages are based on a set hire period, with additional hours available. The right duration depends on your event and guest numbers.

When do we get the photos?

Prints are produced at the event. Digital image delivery and gallery options depend on the package you choose.

Can I get more copies of the pictures?

Guests can enjoy unlimited prints during hire. Ask us about digital copies and online galleries for your event.

How many people can fit in the booth?

Between one and five people can use the booth at a time.

Can event information be printed on the pictures?

Yes. Prints can include text, logos and graphics.

Can we brand the booth for a company event?

Yes. Ask us about magnetic signs, printed banners, cloth banners and vinyl vehicle wraps.

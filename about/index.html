About TaxiSnaps

More features, technology and quality for your money. TaxiSnaps is a full-time photo booth hire company based in the West Midlands.

Photo booth hire with a classic twist

We provide iconic classic taxi photo booths for weddings, corporate events, parties and proms. Our vintage cabs are fully refurbished, with touchscreen controls and modern photography technology inside. The simple package structure lets you choose the hire time you need, with optional extras available for your event.

With the Taxi Photo Booth, guests can enjoy unlimited prints and leave video messages. A dedicated events manager helps oversee the arrangements, and our team delivers, sets up and removes the booth.

Keep the memories

Depending on your package, event photos and video messages can be shared after the event, with online gallery and social media options available. Ask us what is included when you enquire.

# The Evolution of the Classic Photo Booth

<p class="article-meta">4th September 2019</p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Everyone wants to take a selfie. But do you know when photo booths were first created? While we all love to take photos, the history of the classic photo booth is not widely known. It was very different to the one we enjoy today with all of the modern technology that keeps on getting more advanced.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Let’s dive into the evolution of the classic photo booth!</span></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/the-evolution-of-the-classic-photo-booth/01-first-photobooth-1888.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy" />
<h2>How it all began&#8230;</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It all started in 1888. William Pope and Edward Poole submitted the first patent for an automated machine in Baltimore. But the first photo booth that we would recognise today was created in 1923, Russia. This one had a curtain and is the closest idea to the modern version, created by Anatol Josepho. The public experienced the first photo booth two years later when it became available on Broadway, New York City. You could take and print eight photos for just 25 cents. It took around 10 minutes to receive the photographs. With the success of this one, Photomaton Company began to offer photo booths across the country.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">How did they operate? Well, there are a lot of differences in how the classic photo booth operated and the modern version. For example, you would insert the money into the machine and several people could climb in to get their photograph taken. Instead of the digital setup, you can expect today, this one would just have a camera and film processor.</span></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/the-evolution-of-the-classic-photo-booth/02-old-photobooth-vintage.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy" />
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Photobooth were originally created for documentation, especially in the wartime, but this soon evolved to punters gathering with their friends for photo fun. Over the years, several types of photo booths have emerged. For example, in shops around the country, you can use passport photo booths. Just as the name suggests, this one is meant for collecting new passport photos. The specific format is suitable for official documents as long as you follow the instructions. They still have the seat or bench and operate with a camera and you can choose a series of photos.</span></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/the-evolution-of-the-classic-photo-booth/03-photobooth-passports.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy" />

As mentioned, the photo booths that you can enjoy now are a lot more sophisticated. Just like most technology, they have improved over time. Now, you can expect quality photos, fast operation and a whole lot of fun.</span></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/the-evolution-of-the-classic-photo-booth/04-vintage-tee-pee-wedding-photobooth.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy" />

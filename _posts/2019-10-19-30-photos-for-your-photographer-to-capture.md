# 30 Photos for Your Photographer to Capture

<p class="article-meta">19th October 2019</p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Everyone wants to cherish the memories of their wedding day forever. Of course, one of the most popular ways to do this is with a photographer. They follow you around on your special day, using their skills to capture the amazing moments you will look back on in years to come. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">There are some obvious photos you will want, such as your family photo and a snap of you and your spouse on your special day. But there are some photos that a lot of couples forget about that will be great to look back on.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Here are 30 photos for your photographer to capture on your wedding day.</span></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/2020-wedding-trends/09-tipi-weddings-in-west-midlands-tipi-hire-midlands-sami-tipi-SMD-photography-002.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Sami Tipi midlands venue" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Venue</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You want to remember where you tied the knot. Have the photography take shots of the venue and surrounding landscape before everyone starts to arrive. You will be glad you did afterwards! </span></p>
<p>Image Credit:<a href="https://cocoweddingvenues.co.uk/coco_listing/sami-tipi/"> Coco Wedding Venues:</a> Sami Tipi Midlands</p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/02-beide-getting-ready-shot.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Bride getting ready" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Bride Getting Ready</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Getting ready with your best friends your side is often one of the highlights of the big day. A photographer can come into your suite beforehand and capture the moments as they happen.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="http://www.carltonadkins.co.uk/">Carlton Adkins photography</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/03-bride-with-mother.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="bride with mother" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Mother and the Daughter</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">There is nothing more emotional than seeing a mother and the daughter on her wedding day. This is going to make a lovely photo to look back on in years to come, especially when they are getting ready for the big day.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.curiousrosephotography.com">Curious Rose Photography</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/04-groom-getting-ready.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Groom getting ready" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Groom Getting Ready</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Getting ready for the wedding is a big moment for the groom too. Family and friends are all there for support and this is a great time for some photos.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.davidstubbsphotography.co.uk">Carlton Adkins photography</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/05-groom-fathr.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="groom with father" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Father and the Groom</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">There is a special bond between the father and son. A photographer can capture the magic as it unfolds before the big day.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.johncolson.com/">John Coleson</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/06-the-shoes.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="brides shoes photo" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Bride’s Shoes</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Let’s be honest; you probably splashed out on your wedding shoes. But they never get the attention they deserve on your big day. Since they are hidden underneath your dress, be sure to get a photo of them. </span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://heatonweddingphotography.com/">Heaton Wedding Photography</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/07-the-veil.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="the veil" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Veil</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Every bride loves their veil. Ask your photographer to take a photo capturing your veil in all its beauty so that you can admire it in years to come!</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.fionakellyphotography.com/">Fiona Kelly Photography</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/08-the-dress.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="the dress" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Wedding Dress</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The bride always looks so beautiful in her white dress and with lovely flowers. This calls for a photo before everything gets livelier. </span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.davidliebstphotography.com/">David Leibst</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/09-bride-arriving.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="bride arriving" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Bride Arriving</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Are you travelling in style to your wedding? Whether you are in a classic car or a limousine, you have got to capture the moment the bride first steps out. </span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://lauramayphotography.co.uk/">Laura May</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/10-father-of-bride.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="father of bride" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Father of the Bride</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It is an emotional moment when the father gives away bride before walking down the aisle. This will make a beautiful snapshot for the wedding album.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.ambermariephotography.co.uk/">Amber Marie</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/11-groom-waiting.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="groom waiting" loading="lazy" />
<h2>The Groom Waiting</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The groom will be nervous waiting on his bride walking down the aisle. This will be a moment that some grooms forget. A photo will be the perfect reminder.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://pauljosephphotography.co.uk/">Paul Joseph photography</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/12-walking-down-aisle.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="walking down aisle" loading="lazy" />
<h2>Walking Down the Aisle</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Sometimes walking down the aisle can be a blur. It is a big moment that can be emotional and this means your photographer has got to capture the moment everyone sees you.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.saywellhq.co.uk/">Andy Saywell</a></p>

<h2>The Groom Seeing the Bride</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It is a loving moment when the groom sees the bride for the first time. That look can only be captured by a professional.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://christinemcnally.co.uk">Christine Mcnally Photography</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/14-family-and-friends.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="family and friends" loading="lazy" />
<h2>Shots of Family and Friends</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Taking shots of the family and friends during the ceremony is a good idea. You can see everyone’s reactions, including the parents.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.chrisbarberphotography.co.uk/l">Chris Barber</a></p>

<h2>Exchanging Rings</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">When it is time to exchange the rings, the ceremony really begins. This is a defining moment that you will want to look back on.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="ttps://www.roostain.com/">Roostain</a></p>

<h2>The First Kiss</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The first kiss as husband and wife is always special. This is going to be one of the most treasured photos in your album.</span></p>

<h2>Walking Down the Aisle Together</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Walking down the aisle after the ceremony is exciting and all of the nerves disappear. It will be full of smiles to capture as you want away surrounded by loved ones.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://cdmiller-photography.co.uk/">CD Miller</a></p>

<h2>Confetti Celebration</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It is time to celebrate tying the knot! This calls for confetti celebrations; the perfect moment to capture with all the colours, smiling faces and joy in the air.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://robandsarahgillespie.co.uk">Rob &amp; Sarah Gillespie</a></p>

<h2>The Group Photo</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The group photo gathers everyone together to remember the special. While smiling, traditional ones are good, do not forget the fun ones too.</span></p>
<p>Image Credit: <a href="https://www.davidliebstphotography.com/">David Leibst</a></p>
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/wedding-archive/30-photos-for-your-photographer-to-capture/20-fun-bridemaid-shot.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="fun bridesmaids shot" loading="lazy" />
<h2>A Fun Bridesmaids Photo</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Everyone loves traditional shots but funny ones are great too. Why not get your bridesmaids to pose and have some fun before or after the ceremony?</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The groom and best man are good friends for life. This calls for a photo to celebrate the bond they have.</span></p>

<h2>The Happy Couple</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">There are not many quiet moments for the couple on their special day. But taking some couple photos is a way to spend some time together.</span></p>

<h2>With Your Pet</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Do you have a furry friend attending your big day? Do not forget to take a photo with your best friend. This is sure to make a cute photo for the album, whether it is posing or an action shot.</span></p>

<h2>Cutting the Cake</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Do you have a special cake for your wedding? Do not forget to take a photo and capture the moment you both cut the first slice.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Everyone loves the speeches. This is a time to gather around and enjoy some jokes, stories and loving memories.</span></p>

<h2>The Dinner</h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Let’s not forget to take photos of the dinner before it is all gone!</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Action shots are always good to capture the moment. This includes a shot of you and your spouse during your first dance.

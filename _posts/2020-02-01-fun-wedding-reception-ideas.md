# 10 Ways to guarantee a fun wedding reception

<p class="article-meta">{{ page.date | date: '%-d %B %Y' }}</p>

The bride and groom always look forward to the ceremony. It is the moment they get to tie the knot with the one they love. Of course, your family and friends love this occasion too, but everyone is looking forward to the wedding reception! This is the time your guests can let loose and have some fun with the new bride and groom.

To guarantee you have an awesome and memorable wedding reception, here are ten tips to remember during your planning.

![Wedding reception candle lighting]({{ '/assets/images/wedding-candle-lighting.jpg' | relative_url }})

## Mix up the seating arrangement

A lot of people assume the seating arrangement is simple, but it can have a huge impact on your guests’ evening. Take your time to think about where you want everyone to sit. Group people who will have fun together, but do not be afraid to mix it up too. For example, seat workmates together or family members near each other, and try to make sure each person knows at least one other couple. That helps everyone feel comfortable and enjoy meeting your other guests. Not all your friends and family will have met before, so plan the seating arrangement carefully.

![Wedding reception champagne]({{ '/assets/images/wedding-champagne.jpg' | relative_url }})

## Celebrate with Champagne!

A good way to start the night on a positive note is to provide a glass of champagne for your guests. It gives them a chance to relax and enjoy your special day. Wedding bars are often expensive for your guests, so let them enjoy a glass to celebrate with you.

![Garden games at a wedding]({{ '/assets/images/wedding-garden-games.png' | relative_url }})

## Book some unique entertainment

Every wedding has music and a dance floor, and guests love to dance the night away. Think about other ways to keep your guests engaged too: a surprise performance from a singer, magician or aerialist, or games that get people laughing and socialising. For an outdoor wedding, we recommend [Garden Games]({{ '/garden-games/' | relative_url }}).

![Late-night wedding snack bar]({{ '/assets/images/wedding-snack-bar.jpg' | relative_url }})

## Offer some late-night snacks!

Food is the way to everyone’s heart. Guests can get hungry after a few drinks and a dance, so make sure there are some late-night snacks on offer. They need not be fancy; pizza, tacos and sausage rolls are sure to go down well at the end of the night.

![Thoughtful wedding favours]({{ '/assets/images/wedding-favours.jpg' | relative_url }})

## Give favours they will appreciate

Thoughtful wedding favours make lovely keepsakes for guests. You do not have to spend a fortune: find something funny, cute or personal. You can personalise favours with your wedding date or initials, or include each guest’s name.

## Tasty dessert options

The best part of dinner is always dessert. Offer great options for your guests, from chocolate cake to berry parfaits. Let guests choose their dessert ahead of the big day so they have something to look forward to and can end their meal on a high.

![Wedding invitation with room for more guests]({{ '/assets/images/invite-more-wedding-guests.jpg' | relative_url }})

## Have an awards ceremony

Speeches are often one of the best parts of the wedding reception. Make them different with an awards ceremony. After the speeches, thank special guests for coming, create fun award categories and perhaps let them take home a trophy or prize.

![Wedding couple's first dance]({{ '/assets/images/first-dance.jpg' | relative_url }})

## Learn a routine

The first dance starts the night. Make it an even bigger surprise for your guests by learning a choreographed routine. It can be a fun moment that gets everyone laughing; just make sure you practise before taking centre stage.

![Wedding reception fireworks display]({{ '/assets/images/wedding-reception-fireworks.jpg' | relative_url }})
